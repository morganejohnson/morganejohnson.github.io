About

I'm a student at the University of Nebraska-Lincoln, double majoring in journalism and advertising and public relations in the College of Journalism and Mass Communications, with a minor in English. I'm drawn to strategic communication: understanding an audience, shaping a message, and choosing the right way to share it, whether that's a photo, a video, a social post, or a piece of legislation.

On campus, I represent my college in the student senate, lead the communications committee for the Association of Students of the University of Nebraska (ASUN), and serve as chapter president of Phi Mu. I've also worked in office administration since 2023 and mentored fellow students through my college. Together, those roles have taught me how to listen, stay organized, and follow through.
