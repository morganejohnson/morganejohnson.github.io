How to play

  • Move the coach with the ← and → arrow keys (or A / D). On a phone, hold the arrow buttons or press and hold on the field.
  • Watch the shadows. A dark spot on the turf shows where each piece of trash will land.
  • Three hits and you’re done. Every hit costs one of your three timeouts.
  • It gets worse. Each 30-second quarter is faster and better aimed than the last. Make it to overtime and you’ll see why nobody survives it.
  • Coach’s mood follows your footwork: he’s happy after a close dodge, permanently yelling the rest of the time, and furious when he gets hit.

Trash Talk is a game from The Daily Nebraskan. It is satire: please don’t throw anything at anyone at Memorial Stadium.
