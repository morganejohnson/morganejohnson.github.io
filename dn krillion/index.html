Nebraska Outlier

Think you know Nebraska? Think again.

Seven prompts. Twenty seconds each. Any right answer scores, but the rarer your answer, the more points you get. Cornfields are easy. Can you think of Carhenge?

Your scores saved on this device

How to play

  1. 1
    Read the prompt. Something like "Name a Nebraska town that isn't Lincoln or Omaha."
  2. 2
    Beat the clock. You have 20 seconds to type one answer. Lock it in early or it locks itself when time runs out.
  3. 3
    Go niche. Every correct answer scores, but the obscure ones score the most. Kearney gets 10 points. Monowi, population 1, gets 100.
  4. 4
    Share and come back tomorrow. The Daily Challenge resets at midnight Central. Practice Mode never runs out.

The rarity scale

    Rarity levels are our editors' estimates of how often people would think of each answer, not measured statistics. Answers are checked against a set list. Spelling slips and plurals are fine, but answers that aren't on the list score 0.
